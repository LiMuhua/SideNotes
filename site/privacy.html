SideNotes Privacy Policy

SideNotes · 中文

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SideNotes desktop application ("SideNotes", "the app") handles your information. SideNotes is a free, open-source app for Windows, developed and maintained by LiMuhua on GitHub (source code). SideNotes has no servers of its own and no user accounts. It does not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, location, contacts or any other usage data.

1. Information SideNotes handles

Notes you create

Your notes, attachments, settings and local snapshots are stored in a data folder on your computer. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on cloud sync.

Google user data (only if you connect Google Drive)

When you choose Google Drive for cloud sync and sign in with Google, SideNotes requests one scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, SideNotes accesses:

  • Your Google account email address, read through the Google Drive API.
  • Only the files and folders that SideNotes creates in your Google Drive: the folder you choose for snapshots and, inside it, your note snapshots — a copy of the notes database, your attachments, and small index files that record each snapshot's time and number of notes.

SideNotes cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

Microsoft account data (only if you connect OneDrive)

SideNotes requests Microsoft Graph Files.ReadWrite, User.Read and offline_access. It reads your account name and reads and writes files only in the remote folder you choose.

Synology NAS (only if you connect one)

SideNotes connects over WebDAV with the address, user name and password you enter, and reads and writes files only in the remote folder you choose.

Credentials

To keep cloud sync working, SideNotes stores sign-in tokens (OAuth refresh tokens), your NAS password and, if you set one, your Resend API key.

2. How SideNotes uses information

SideNotes uses Google user data only to provide the cloud sync features that you see and control in the app:

  • to upload snapshots of your notes to your chosen folder when you click "Sync now" or when automatic sync runs on the schedule you set;
  • to list and download your snapshots when you restore your notes;
  • to delete old snapshots from that folder according to the retention rule below;
  • to show your Google account email address in Settings, so you can see which account is connected.

SideNotes does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, market research, or training artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and no person reads it.

3. Sharing and disclosure

SideNotes does not sell, rent, trade or share your data. Data travels only between your computer and the services you connect yourself (Google, Microsoft or your NAS), over encrypted HTTPS connections. There is no SideNotes server in between.

The only exception is an optional feature you set up yourself: if you enter a recipient email address and your own Resend API key, SideNotes sends you an email through Resend (resend.com) when a sync fails. That email contains the service name, the connected account (for Google Drive, your Google account email address), the remote folder, the error message and the time. Nothing is sent to Resend unless you set this up.

4. Storage and security

  • Your notes and settings stay in the data folder on your computer.
  • Sign-in tokens, the NAS password and the Resend API key are encrypted with Windows DPAPI before they are saved, so only the same Windows user on the same computer can read them. They are never written to the settings and are not included in local backups.
  • Short-lived access tokens are requested when needed and kept only in memory.
  • All network connections use HTTPS.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your local notes stay on your computer until you delete them.
  • In the cloud folder, SideNotes keeps every snapshot from the last 24 hours, one snapshot per day for the last 7 days, one per week for the last 35 days, and always the latest 3; it deletes older snapshots automatically.
  • Click "Sign out" or "Disconnect" in Settings to delete the stored credentials. For Google Drive, SideNotes also revokes its access token with Google.
  • Delete the remote folder in your Google Drive, OneDrive or NAS to delete all cloud snapshots.
  • You can revoke SideNotes's access at any time in your Google Account's third-party apps & services or your Microsoft account's apps and services.
  • Uninstalling SideNotes and deleting its data folder removes all local data.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

SideNotes's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, SideNotes uses Google user data only to provide user-facing features that are prominent in the app; transfers it only as needed to provide those features (the optional failure email you set up), to comply with law, or for security; does not use it for advertising; and does not allow humans to read it.

7. Children

SideNotes is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, please open an issue on GitHub Issues.
