SideNotes

Quick notes in a sidebar for Windows · 中文

SideNotes is a free, open-source note-taking app for Windows. Move the pointer to the edge of the screen, or press a shortcut, to slide out a sidebar; jot down to-dos, commands, links, ideas and screenshots; and the sidebar slides away again so you can get back to work. Your notes are stored on your own computer.

What SideNotes does

  • Keeps your notes in a local database on your computer. No account or sign-up is required.
  • Lets you drag a note out of the sidebar into its own window, keep it on top, or group notes as tabs.
  • Offers five themes — Terminal, Magazine, Paper, Windows 11 and Frosted glass — each in light and dark.
  • Takes a local snapshot of your notes every day, and restores any snapshot.
  • Optional cloud sync: stores snapshots of your notes in storage you own — a Synology NAS (WebDAV), Microsoft OneDrive or Google Drive — and restores any of them.

How SideNotes uses Google user data

SideNotes accesses Google Drive only if you choose Google Drive for cloud sync and sign in. It requests a single scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which lets it access only the files and folders that SideNotes itself creates in your Google Drive. SideNotes uses this access to:

  • create the folder you choose for your snapshots, and upload snapshots of your notes and attachments into it when you sync;
  • list and download those snapshots when you restore your notes;
  • delete old snapshots from that folder according to the retention rule;
  • read your Google account's email address, to show in Settings which account is connected.

SideNotes cannot see any other files in your Google Drive. Data travels directly between your computer and Google: SideNotes has no servers, and it does not sell, share or use Google user data for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for details.
